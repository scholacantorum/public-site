**Messiah Sing Comes Home for Its 60th Year**

The Bay Area’s longest-running Messiah Sing is coming home.
For our 60th, Schola Cantorum returns to the Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts,
the stage where this holiday tradition lived for decades.
Lockdown sent us online, then outdoors, and we couldn’t be happier to be back.

MVCPA is the perfect home for a Messiah Sing:
room for a full Baroque orchestra with continuo, hundreds of choral voices, and a lineup of soloists.
Who are the singers and soloists? You are! You sing the choruses, and you sing the solos.

Bring your score, your voice, and your holiday spirit, and help us celebrate our diamond anniversary: 60 years of singing together.

Get your tickets from the [MVCPA box office](https://tickets.mvcpa.com/eventperformances.asp?evt=801), on sale now through Dec 21!
