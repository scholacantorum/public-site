**Schola’s *Messiah Sing*** is the oldest in the Bay Area, and we are returning to our favorite venue for this, our 60th iteration. Make plans to be at the **Mountain View Center for the Performing Arts** on **Monday, December 21 at 7:30 pm**. We presented this favorite holiday event for decades at MVCPA. Lockdown forced us online, then outdoors... we are thrilled to be back “home.”

It’s a great venue for Schola’s *Messiah Sing*, worthy of the Baroque-scale full orchestra, with continuo, and the hundreds of choral singers and soloists — why, of course, that’s all of you! You sing the choruses and you sing the solos.

Tickets, through the [MVCPA box office](https://www.mvcpa.com/visit/ticket-services), will go on sale this fall.
