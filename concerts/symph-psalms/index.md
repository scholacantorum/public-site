Schola is thrilled to once again join forces with **Redwood Symphony**.
We’ll sing the ***Symphony of Psalms*** for Redwood Symphony’s
**Stravinsky Festival**, a two-concert presentation of nine works from this
transformative composer. It’s a three-movement choral symphony composed in 1930,
during his neoclassical period. Its religious character emerges through fugal
counterpoint, large choral forces and the use of psalms as texts. Stravinsky
described the work as psalms transformed into symphonic music, and infused with
profound spiritual intensity.

_This concert is hosted by Redwood Symphony, not by Schola Cantorum, and is not
included in a Schola Cantorum FlexPASS.  Tickets will be sold on the
[Redwood Symphony website](https://redwoodsymphony.org/concert/a-rite-of-spring-with-redwood-symphony/)._
