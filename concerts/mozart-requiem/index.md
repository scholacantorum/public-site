Our season’s grandest evening brings choir and full orchestra together for two sacred masterworks. Mozart’s soaring _Requiem_, his final and unfinished masterpiece, comes alive in all its richness, with every swell of strings and blaze of brass, alongside Brahms’s radiant _Geistliches Lied_. It’s an evening of glorious, enveloping sound you’ll feel as much as hear.
